Privacy policy

Updated: 12 November 2026

Accounts and data

AnyCoffee requires no account. This version has no sign-in.

The app does not collect personal data, include usage analytics or advertising, or send data out. It works offline.

Your equipment, setups, brews, saved recipes, notes, language and settings stay on your device. They are not synced.

Deleting data

In the app, go to Profile → Data on this device to delete individual data types or choose Delete all. Uninstalling the app also deletes all its data.

Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may log technical data, such as your IP address, under its privacy policy.

Policy updates

This policy may change when the app gains new features. The update date appears at the top of this page.
